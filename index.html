Overview

What can the app do?

Demo Travels App is a complete back office for a travel agency. It keeps every client, booking, bill and payment connected, so balances and reports are always up to date. You never have to add anything up by hand.

👥

Clients & agents

Keep contact details, see what each person owes and record their payments.
🎫

Bookings

Flights, trains, buses, hotels, visas, packages and medical appointments.
🧾

Bills & invoices

Turn a booking into a bill in one tap. Print it, save a PDF or send it on WhatsApp.
🧳

Group packages

Plan tours with passengers, transport, hotels, invoices and supplier settlements.
📊

Reports

Receivables, margins, profit and client or provider statements.
🏦

Cash & bank

Track money in cash and bank accounts, including transfers and office expenses.

Getting started

The app runs in any modern web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There's nothing to install.

Log in

  1. Open the app in your browser.
  2. Type your Mobile Number. You can enter it with or without +91, spaces or a leading 0.
  3. Type your Password. Tap the 👁 icon if you want to check what you typed.
  4. Press Log In. You'll land on the Dashboard.

Finding your way around

  • Left menu (on a computer): every section of the app, one click away. The highlighted item is where you are.
  • ☰ Menu button (on a phone): opens the same menu from the left.
  • Top bar: shows the page name, today's date and who is logged in.
  • Search bars and filter chips: on almost every list, to find records quickly.

Who can see what?

There are two kinds of user. Admins see everything. Staff handle daily work, but money-sensitive areas are hidden from them.

Section 4

Clients

All users

Your customer list. Each client shows how much they've been billed and whether they're settled or still owe money.

Add a client

  1. Open Clients and tap ➕ Add New Client.
  2. Fill in the Client Name and Phone (pick the country code from the list). Email, address and notes are optional.
  3. If the client already owed you money before you started using the app, enter it as the Opening Balance.
  4. Press Save.

See a client's details and take a payment

  1. Tap a client to open their card. It shows Total Billed, Total Paid, the Balance Due and all their bookings.
  2. Press Record Payment to take money against their bills. The amount is applied to their oldest bills first, and you can see a live preview before saving.
  3. Use Payment History to see, edit or delete earlier receipts, or Edit to change their details.
🔒
Safe deleteA client with bookings can't be deleted, so no history is lost by accident.
🤝
Section 5

B2B Agents

All users

Other travel agents you work with. An agent can be your customer (you sell to them) and your supplier (you buy from them), and the app tracks both sides.

  • Add an agent with ➕ Add New B2B Agent. The details are the same as for a client.
  • When a booking is made for an agent, its bill goes to the agent instead of the traveller.
  • Open an agent to see Purchased from Agent, Paid to Agent and the Balance We Owe. Record supplier payments from there.
🏢
Section 6

Service Providers

All users

The airlines, hotels, visa suppliers, train agents and consolidators you buy from. The app shows what you owe each one.

  1. Tap ➕ Add Service Provider, enter the name and tick every Type they supply (for example, Airline + Consolidator).
  2. Add a contact person, phone, email, GSTIN and an opening balance if you already owe them money.
  3. When you pay a provider, press Record Payment and choose the Cash or Bank account it came from.
  4. Press History to see every purchase and payment for that provider.
🎫
Section 7

Bookings

All users

Every ticket, visa, hotel stay or appointment you sell starts as a booking.

Create a booking

  1. Open Bookings and tap ➕ New Booking.
  2. Booking For: choose Client or B2B Agent, then pick who it's for.
  3. Buy from: choose the Provider (or a B2B agent) you're buying from.
  4. Pick the Type, then fill in the PNR / booking reference, passenger name(s), route or country, and the travel date.
  5. Enter the Cost Price (what you pay) and the Selling Price (what the client pays). The margin is worked out for you.
  6. Press Save.

Turn it into a bill

Each unbilled booking has a blue Generate Bill button. Tap it and a numbered bill (for example DT-0007) is created straight away. The booking then shows a green Billed badge.

💡
One booking, one billThe app never creates two bills for the same booking, even if you double-tap.
🧾
Section 8

Bills & payments

All users

All your bills in one list, each with a clear payment status.

StatusMeaning
UnpaidNothing has been paid yet.
Partially PaidSome money has been received and a balance is still due.
PaidFully settled.

Record a payment

  1. Tap a bill to open it.
  2. Press Record Payment.
  3. Enter the amount, the date, and the Cash or Bank account the money went into. You can't enter more than the amount due.
  4. Press Save. The status and all the totals update right away.

Share the invoice

  • Download / Print PDF opens your browser's print window. Choose Save as PDF to keep a copy.
  • 💬 Send via WhatsApp creates the invoice PDF. On a phone it opens the share sheet. On a computer it downloads the file so you can attach it in WhatsApp.
📄
Section 9

The invoice

Every bill prints as a clean, branded invoice that's ready to send to your client.

  • Letterhead: your logo, address, phone and email.
  • Bill To: the client's name and contact details, plus the invoice number and date.
  • Details: passengers, route, travel date and PNR.
  • Totals: bill amount, amount paid and the balance due (in red while money is still owed).
  • Bank details: taken from the bank account you mark as Default on invoices in Accounts.
  • Seal and signature: added automatically.
↩️
Section 10

Cancellations & refunds

All users

When a client cancels a booking that's already been billed, record a refund so the numbers stay right.

  1. In Bookings, find the billed booking and press the red Refund button.
  2. Enter the Refund Amount going back to the client. The rest is kept as your Penalty, and the app shows it as you type.
  3. If the client had already paid more than the penalty, choose the account the refund is paid from.
  4. Save. The booking is marked Refunded, and the bill, the client's balance and your profit all adjust automatically.
⚠️
Refunds are finalA refund is a one-time action for a booking, so check the amount before saving.
📜
Section 11

Attestation

All users

Track document attestation jobs (education, marriage, birth, employment, commercial and more) from start to finish.

  1. Tap ➕ Add Attestation and choose the Client, Country and Category. If your category isn't listed, add a custom one.
  2. Choose the supplier and enter the cost and selling prices.
  3. Update the Status as the job moves along: Processing → In Transfer → Approved → In Transfer Back → Completed. You can also mark it Rejected or Cancelled.
🧳
Section 12

Packages (group tours)

All users

Plan a complete group trip, such as Umrah, Hajj, Lakshadweep or a domestic or international tour, and track every rupee in and out.

Create a package

  1. Tap ➕ Create Event. Enter a title, the trip type, from and to dates, the maximum number of people and the travel modes.
  2. Set the Status: Planning, Confirmed, Completed or Cancelled.
  3. Open the package to manage it using the tabs.
TabWhat you do there
PassengersAdd travellers one by one or as a group, with passport number, room sharing and package price. Record each passenger's payments.
Transport · HotelsAdd flights, buses and so on (with PNR and times) and hotel stays, each with its supplier and cost.
Purchases · ExpensesRecord extras such as visas, food, guides, local transport and tips.
SettlementSee what you owe each supplier and record payments to them.
Create InvoicePick passengers (for example, one family) and produce a single invoice for them. Earlier invoices can be reprinted from Invoice History.

Accounts (Cash & Bank)

Admin only

Every payment you receive or make goes into a Cash or Bank account, so you always know how much money is where.

  • A Cash account is created for you automatically. Add bank accounts under the Bank tab.
  • Mark one bank as Default on invoices. Its details are printed on every invoice.
  • ⇄ Transfer Funds moves money Cash → Bank or Bank → Cash, for example when you deposit the day's cash.
  • Open an account to see its full Transaction History and running balance for any period.

Expenses

Admin only

Record office running costs so your profit picture is complete.

  1. Tap ➕ Add Expense.

  3. Save. The Office Expenses Report at the bottom totals everything for the period, and you can print it.

Manage users

Admin only

FAQ & troubleshooting

I can't log in, and it says "Incorrect phone number or password".
Check the mobile number (with or without +91 is fine) and the password. Passwords are case-sensitive. If you still can't get in, ask an admin to reset your password from Manage Users. Your account may also have been disabled.
Why don't I see Accounts, Expenses or profit figures?
Those are only for admins. See Who can see what.
I made a mistake in a booking. Can I change it?
Yes. Tap the booking to edit it. Once a booking has been refunded it's locked, but you can still open it to view the details.
How do I record a part payment?
Open the bill, press Record Payment and enter the amount received. The bill moves to Partially Paid and shows the balance due. Record the rest later in the same way.
A client paid for several bills at once. What do I do?
Open the client in Clients and press Record Payment. One amount is spread across their bills, oldest first, and you'll see a preview before you save.
How do I save an invoice as a PDF?
Open the bill, press Download / Print PDF, and in the print window choose Save as PDF as the printer.
Can I delete a bill?
Yes. Open the bill and use the delete (🗑) button. This removes the bill and all its payments and can't be undone, so use it only for mistakes.
A page is stuck on "Loading…".
Check your internet connection, then refresh the page. If a page shows an error, press Retry.
